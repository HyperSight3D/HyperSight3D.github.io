Lab Introduction

HyperSight 3D Perception Lab is affiliated with the School of Software at Dalian University of Technology. Our lab is dedicated to cutting-edge research in 3D perception, computer vision, and artificial intelligence.

We focus on developing advanced algorithms and systems for 3D scene understanding, object detection, point cloud processing, and multi-modal perception. Our research has wide applications in autonomous driving, robotics, augmented reality, and smart manufacturing.

The lab is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities including high-performance computing clusters, various sensors (LiDAR, RGB-D cameras, stereo cameras), and robotic platforms.

Research Interests

Stereo Vision

Research on stereo matching, depth estimation, 3D reconstruction, and real-time stereo perception systems. Our work focuses on developing robust algorithms for accurate depth perception in various environments, with applications in autonomous driving, robotics, and augmented reality.

Multi-modal Perception

Development of multi-sensor fusion algorithms combining RGB, infrared, LiDAR, and other modalities. We focus on cross-modal learning, sensor calibration, and robust perception systems that can operate in challenging conditions such as low-light, fog, and adverse weather.

Computational Imaging

Research on advanced imaging techniques including event-based vision, high-speed imaging, and computational photography. Our work explores novel camera architectures, image enhancement algorithms, and physics-based imaging simulation for next-generation vision systems.
